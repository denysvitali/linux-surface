# SPX audio QMI power request — completed result, 2026-09-22

Trial `20260922h` successfully sent the ACTIVE SLIMbus QMI power request,
received `result=0x0 error=0x0`, and stopped at the intended stage-2 guard.
ADSP, the desktop, Wi-Fi and IPTSD remained running through the completed
observation. The modem started once without an announcement failure or fatal
error.

The finalized result records **865.10 seconds uptime**, exceeding the required
600 seconds; the archived collector reached 868.37 seconds. An independent
live review at 785.04–785.16 seconds confirmed the guards and services, and a
kernel fault scan remained clear at 963.32 seconds. Mainline was intentionally
retained after archive verification; no recovery return was performed.
No soundcard is expected at this stage. Physical touch/pen and audio playback
remain untested.

## Other startup observations

kernel created virtual touchscreen/stylus devices at 28.008044/28.009154
seconds. The daemon remained active with its normal in-tree UINPUT support;

retries at approximately 27.46–27.85 seconds before this successful open.
Virtual-device creation does not establish physical touch or pen operation.

The modem reported up at 28.651788 seconds. Ath10k QMI initialization followed
at 29.377254 seconds and WCN3990 initialization at 32.721251 seconds. WPA/CCMP
negotiation completed at 43.201458 seconds, DHCP at 44.028522 seconds and
NetworkManager activation at 44.113162 seconds. No modem announcement failure
or fatal error appeared through the completed observation. This is another
clean startup with G's QRTR rollback, not proof of complete modem functionality
or resolution of an intermittent race.

GNOME started as a Wayland server at 30.598224 seconds, created its GBM
renderer at 31.672408 seconds and reported shell startup at 42.015044 seconds.
EGL identified freedreno FD680 with OpenGL 4.6 and OpenGL ES 3.2 at
119.387064 seconds. No HFI timeout, GMU watchdog, GPU hangcheck, oops or panic
was observed, nor a kernel WARN stack trace, lockup or ADSP failure. GDM, iwd,
NetworkManager and IPTSD remained active, and Wi-Fi remained connected. The
governor was `simple_ondemand`, internal eDP connected, and external DP disconnected.
