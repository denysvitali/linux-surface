# SPX audio version-read trial I — final scoped result, 2026-09-22

Trial I passed the bounded stage-3 audio observation through **612.76 seconds
uptime**: the version read returned the expected value, the explicit stop
completed, and audio guards and remote processors remained intact. Desktop
graphics and Wi-Fi remained operational. Touch/pen startup failed and was
recovered by one ordinary daemon restart; this is not an input-startup pass.

this document was finalized. Mainline was intentionally retained; no
recovery return occurred.

## Desktop, network and input

GDM, iwd and NetworkManager were active; `wlan0` was connected. The existing
graphics check at **119.919920 seconds** reported EGL 1.5, hardware renderer
**FD680**, OpenGL 4.6 and OpenGL ES 3.2. Modem, CDSP and ADSP were running;
the modem's single start completed at **29.217333 seconds**. Kernel taint
was zero. The finalized archived journal contained no modem announcement
failure or fatal, GPU/HFI/GMU fault, kernel oops or panic during the bounded
observation.

**Touch/pen startup failed on I.** `iptsd@dev-hidraw4.service` started at
28.244575 seconds, then exited with status 1 at 30.305078. Its error at
30.307486 states that opening `/dev/hidraw4` timed out. The kernel logged
SPI-HID invalid-header errors (`-22`) at 28.296442 and 28.812784, with error
handler attempts 0 and 1. The instantiated service was still failed and no
IPTSD virtual touch/pen devices appeared in `/proc/bus/input/devices`.
The raw SPI stylus and keyboard/touchpad input nodes existed, which does not
prove touch/pen functionality. Physical interaction was not tested.

This is a separate observed failure; its cause and any relation to the
audio stage are not established.

Root preserved the original state under I's `touch-recovery/` archive, then
performed one ordinary daemon restart at **239.088593 seconds**. It reported
a successful firmware open at 239.297218, connection at 239.321115 and
virtual touch/pen creation at 239.321350/239.321448. There was one additional
SPI-HID header-recovery attempt at 239.153867. The independent follow-up at
**290.77 seconds** verified the daemon active and both `IPTSD Virtual
Touchscreen 045E:0C21` and `IPTSD Virtual Stylus 045E:0C21` present. The audio
child remained suspended with runtime-active time zero. No audio or device
unbind/rebind occurred. This is successful service recovery, not successful
input startup or a physical gesture test; no restart policy had been changed
when these observations were recorded.
