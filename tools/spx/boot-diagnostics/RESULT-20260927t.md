The skipped devices have SoundWire IDs `0217:2110` because both DT speaker
nodes declare `sdw10217211000`. The force-attach worker filters for
`0217:2010`, which is the physical DevID recorded in the DT comments and
the driver's diagnostic reads. The WSA881x codec driver accepts both IDs.
The next isolated experiment changes the two DT compatible strings to
`sdw10217201000`, matching the physical ID and the worker filter. A decompiled
old/new DTB comparison shows exactly those two property changes. Keep the
same kernel, modules, initramfs and command-line audio selectors; use a
separate U archive, one-shot boot and result collector. Logical attachment
alone cannot prove audible playback.
