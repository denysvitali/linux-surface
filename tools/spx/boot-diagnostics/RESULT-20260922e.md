# SPX extended graphical and CMAC baseline — 2026-09-22

Trial `20260922e` passed its extended graphical baseline and remained running
on mainline at the recorded 827.19-second checkpoint (13 minutes 47 seconds).
Wayland EGL identified the hardware renderer as freedreno FD680, iwd started
with CMAC support, and NetworkManager connected Wi-Fi through wpa_supplicant.
The archived baseline contains no GPU/HFI failure, kernel oops/panic or modem
fatal error.

The mainline session was intentionally retained. **No recovery reboot was
performed in E**, and this result does not claim automatic recovery or complete
port validation. Subsequent live component experiments are separate from this
baseline; physical touchscreen and pen operation had not been verified.

## Retained session and remaining work

The original ten-minute return timer was replaced during live validation by
a transient fallback scheduled for approximately 842 seconds uptime. After
the baseline passed at 827.19 seconds, that fallback was stopped and the
mainline session was retained. The journal records the stop at 827.401
seconds; `result.json` explicitly records `recovery_return_performed: false`.
The persistent known-good selection was preserved. E therefore adds no new
evidence about recovery across the EFI handoff or automatic agent resumption.

No HFI timeout, GMU watchdog expiry, GPU hangcheck failure, kernel oops/panic
or modem fatal error was found in the archived baseline. The modem result
differs from D's repeated crashes, but E includes no QRTR fix and no cellular
connection test; the absence of a crash in this interval does not establish
the cause or a complete modem fix.

IPTSD failed opening `/dev/uinput` at 28.268 seconds. Its SPI endpoint reached
the daemon, but E lacks `CONFIG_INPUT_UINPUT`, so no successful virtual
touchscreen/stylus setup or physical touch/pen result belongs to this baseline.
The board fragment carries `CONFIG_INPUT_UINPUT=m` for a future normal build.
Any supplementary module insertion and daemon restart after this checkpoint
must retain its own command, artifact and runtime evidence.

Audio still has no registered sound card and `spx-audio-modules.service`
failed at 38.241 seconds. SLIMbus remains under its stage-0 guard. External
DisplayPort, speakers, cameras, physical touch/pen, sustained GPU/Wi-Fi load
and suspend/resume remain unvalidated by this result.

## Separate live UINPUT follow-up

After the baseline checkpoint, the unchanged E `uinput.c` and
`input-compat.h` were built as a supplementary external module against the
exact running E build. `live-uinput/` archives the source, build command/log,
module, manifest, load intent and service restart evidence. The module's
SHA-256 is
`4e87da8c6d78bbea7e03c6bd49d1a373b4d07905ee03ecd16ec1c5f2c56a89fa`,
verified against the archived file; its vermagic matches E. The original
kernel config and Image were retained.

The module loaded at 889.004 seconds and recorded the expected out-of-tree
module taint. Only the IPTSD service was restarted, at 889.085 seconds. It
reported connection to `045E:0C21` in Touchscreen mode at 889.278 seconds;
the kernel created `IPTSD Virtual Touchscreen 045E:0C21` and
`IPTSD Virtual Stylus 045E:0C21` as input23 and input24. Read-only checks
confirmed the node identities and active daemon.

This confirms that supplying UINPUT resolves E's captured daemon startup
failure. It does not verify physical touch/pen accuracy, gesture behavior or
long-term SPI-HID reliability. The supplementary module is a separate runtime
change after the immutable baseline, not part of E's original build payload.
