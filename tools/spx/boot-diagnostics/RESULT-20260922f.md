# SPX persistent UINPUT and audio stage 1 — result, 2026-09-22

Trial `20260922f` completed an 813.84-second observation on mainline, exceeding
the planned ten-minute window. Its graphical desktop remained running, the
normal in-tree UINPUT module automatically supported IPTSD's virtual touch/pen
devices, and audio stage 1 successfully selected the ADSP SLIMbus instance,
pinned the module and stopped at the intended guard. ADSP remained running.

These are bounded desktop, input-daemon and audio-handshake successes. The
modem still failed once during startup, then recovered. Physical touch/pen
operation and audio playback are not passed. The session was intentionally
retained on mainline after archiving the result; **no recovery reboot was
performed in F**.

## Other observations and open checks

Wayland EGL again reported freedreno FD680 with OpenGL 4.6 and OpenGL ES 3.2
at 120.714 seconds. The GPU used `simple_ondemand`; eDP was connected and
external DP disconnected. No HFI/GMU watchdog, GPU hangcheck, oops or panic
was found through the completed observation. GDM, iwd, NetworkManager and IPTSD
were active at the final check. Wi-Fi completed WPA/CCMP negotiation at
85.156 seconds and NetworkManager activation at 86.078 seconds; it remained
connected and carried the session after the modem restart. This does not
replace a sustained transfer or reconnection test.

At 364.12–365.13 seconds, a small write through the normal backlight class
changed brightness from 1864 to 1762 and restored 1864. Both requested and
`actual_brightness` readbacks matched the target and restored value, without
component rebinding. `brightness-check.json` records this control-path
round-trip. No human visual change was confirmed, so physical brightness
response and display blank/unblank remain unverified.

The modem first came up at 29.909084 seconds, immediately followed by
`failed to announce new service` at 29.909858 seconds. It reported a fatal
error at 69.894706 seconds and restarted successfully at 70.533028 seconds.
No additional modem fatal error appeared through the 813.84-second checkpoint.
This remains an unresolved modem fault; the timing does not establish that the audio QMI
selection caused it. Similar modem faults occurred before the stage-1 trial.

The replacement fallback was stopped after the archive completed; the journal
records that stop at 815.083 seconds. `result.json` explicitly records no
recovery return, and the persistent known-good default is unchanged. Agent
continuation also remains separate: the archived manual acknowledgment says
the user supplied Continue and automatic prompt delivery was not verified.
