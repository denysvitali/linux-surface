commands, while APR preserves nonzero identities for Windows-compatible
clients. The initial PAUSE reply went to unregistered service 16 and timed
out. A narrow ASM-only live correction made PAUSE/FLUSH/CLOSE acknowledge.
The permanent q6asm fix builds and is installed for the next candidate boot;
the module is absent from the initramfs. The live correction remains active.

**Meaning:** reset suppression, clock restoration, flush and native ASM
restart are insufficient. After restart the preserved-route shortcut skips
necessary matrix reconnection; native remapping restores DSP consumption,
proving the retained memory mapping still works, but does not restore sound.
Old claims that ASM CLOSE can never acknowledge are disproved. This does
not establish safe UNMAP or AFE STOP. RX0 overflow is a known self-masking
boundary flag, also observed during audible output, not proof of corruption.
