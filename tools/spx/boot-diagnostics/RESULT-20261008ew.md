# Trial 20261008ew: front OV5693, IR OV7251 and rear OV13858 in the mainline graph

Boot `9366f2e2`, release `7.3.0-rc2-spx-mainline-20261004bg+`, DTB `23848bdb15dd`
(front and IR DT), libcamera step and dma-buf config (`9456eaf0`).

**Changed:** front OV5693 (CCI0 bus 1, MCLK2/GPIO15, reset GPIO12) and IR OV7251
(CCI0 bus 0, MCLK3/GPIO16, enable GPIO23) added to the DT, all three sensor
modules and dma-buf in the trial kernel.

**Observed:**
- camss, CSIPHY and CCI bound. The IR OV7251 probed at `4-0060` (revision 7).
- Front OV5693 at `5-0036` failed: `i2c-qcom-cci ac4a000.cci: master 1 queue 0
  timeout`, then `Error reading reg 0x300a: -110`.
- Rear OV13858 and IR links are missing from the media graph. Capture result:
  front "sensor is not in the camss graph"; rear and IR "graph is missing the
  sensor link". libcamera did not run.
- Return to known-good at 900 s: boot `839a0b36`, `next_entry` empty,
  known-good payloads intact.

**Meaning:** the front sensor fails on CCI bus 1 before identification, which
is a sensor-side fault. The IR sensor probes but has no graph link, and the
rear sensor logs no probe at all. The likely common cause is that camss does
not complete its sensor bind while the front endpoint fails; this is not yet
tested.
