MCLK2 are on, so the remaining I2C-level causes are: the pin identity or
electrical state of gpio19/20 on this board, the reset release sequence, or
the sensor's address on bus 1.

**Refuted:** "CCI bus 1 pins missing" (as a sufficient cause).
