# WW: microphone verifies speaker close/reopen with retained RX clocks

Boot `74d5cfa5-85fe-476f-ad27-8f3e53b4e8d3`, kernel
`7.3.0-rc2-spx-mainline-20261004bg+`. Started in multi-user mode after WV
ended before its test. Baseline completed and journal sync succeeded. WW
remained available throughout testing; the earlier reset cause is unknown.

**Changed:** loaded `spx_audio_rx_hold` before any PCM. Both INT7/8 supplies
were On (forced). Native COMP7/8 off controls changed hardware bits from
2 to 0 while amplifier enables stayed low. No manual register correction.
Ran the unchanged quiet microphone reopen test, peak 1433 and minimum PA gain.

**Observed:** all six acoustic phases detected their expected tones across
two distinct playback owners, with closed status between them. First left
and right were 2.607 and 20.010 RMS; reopened left and right were 2.626 and
20.193, against their respective 0.248 and 0.160 tone-band noise references.
Both-channel phases also detected both tones. DSP consumed 332/332 and
325/325 buffers, zero fallback. Capture was 1,973,760 frames; raw samples
discarded. All PCMs closed; GPIO 0x1e/0x18, FIFO zero, no cleanup errors.

**Meaning:** holding the speaker interpolator supplies continuously prevents
the acoustic reopen defect on this configuration. The native compander
control correction is hardware-verified. This is not full A01 acceptance:
useful volume, physical mapping/balance and broader lifecycle remain open.

**Integration:** the codec probe now forces only INT7_CLK/INT8_CLK when SPX
retained playback is enabled. ASoC applies the votes at initial card sync;
amplifier power-down remains unchanged. The native module builds, passes
checkpatch, and is installed in the candidate module tree. Its probe
activation still needs the next normal mainline boot.
The live boot keeps the verified helper loaded; replacing the codec would
destroy retained DSP mappings, so no extra reboot or card reload was done.
